1950s Fashion

PNG File

The 1950s American ideal was hyperfeminine, luxurious and excessive. The New Look, created by Christian Dior pushed the exaggerated hourglass figure that we recognize from the 50s into the mainstream. After years of postwar austerity, it was a celebration of excess. In the 40s fabric had to be conserved for the war effort, leading to the popularity of pencil skirts, but in the 50s, loosening fabric restrictions meant that people could afford the luxury of full skirts. The dress I chose is called Venus, from Dior’s Autumn/Winter 1949s collection. It’s a perfect demonstration of the excess, unabashed femininity that characterized the 1950s.
