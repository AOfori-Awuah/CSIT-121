Single Serve Sticky Toffee Pudding

This is a simple sticky toffee pudding recipe made for a 500 ml ramekin. It's a perfect, cozy treat
for a cold day. It makes two small servings or one large one. I recommend serving with ice cream or a dollup of whipped cream.

Ingredients

For the Pudding

  • 40-50 grams pitted Medjool dates (about 3-4)
  • !/4 cup boiling water
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp dark maple syrup
  • 1/2 vanilla extract
  • 1/2 egg
  • 1/3 cup all purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  • a pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp milk

For the Toffee Sauce

  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp dark brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp dark maple syrup
  • 1 pinch salt

(If you would like extra toffee sauce for serving, multiply this by 1.5)

Instructions

  1. Set out milk, egg, and maple syrup and allow to come to room temperature.
  2. Chop dates finely. Add to a bowl and cover with boiling water and baking soda.
    Make sure dates are submerged in the water. Allow to sit and soften.
  3. Grease your ramekin and preheat your oven to 350 degrees.
  4. Add flour, baking powder, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt into a bowl and whisk to combine.
  5. Over a bain marie, melt your butter. Remove the butter from heat and whisk in brown sugar
    rigourously until dissolved. Add your vanilla, maple syrup, egg, and milk.
  6. Add date mixture to wet ingredients, stir to combine.
  7. Combine wet and dry ingredients.
  8. Pour into ramekin and bake until a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs, 20-25 minutes,
  9. Prepare your toffee sauce while the pudding is in the oven. Over a bain marie melt butter, then
    whisk in brown sugar, salt, and maple syrup until dissolved.
  10. When the pudding is done, poke deep holes in it and pour toffee sauce over it. Allow the pudding
    to completely absorb the sauce before serving.
  11. Top with ice cream or whipped cream and enjoy!
