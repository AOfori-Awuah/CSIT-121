Labs 1 and 2

What I Learned

  1. Basic HTML tags and formatting. Though we haven't yet explored the deeper and more complex aspects of HTML,these projects have introduced me to the language and increased my literacy.   2. File management and formatting. I've felt for a long time that I struggled to organize my files in ways that made them easy to navigate and edit, but now I'm being more intentional about the way I organize and name my files.

Lab 3

What I Learned

  1. Basics of CSS rules. Learning about these have allowed me to add a new layer of customization and complexity to my projects. Being able to add colors, fonts, and other element specifications has made my project much more visually interesting and complex. I'm able to see simple lines of text on a page transform into what looks like an actual webpage I might find online. Though I still don't have the interactivity that would come with adding javascript, I feel that I have a much more robust understanding of webpage structure.

The downside of that freedom is that it also means that I made more mistakes that took a longer time to debug, but the process of debugging has strengthened my knowledge.
